# Internship Report – Specialized Topic 2

## 📘 Project Title:
**Fabrication and Optical Characterization of ZnO Thin Films Using the Sol-Gel Method**

---

## 📌 Purpose of the Report

This report presents the process and results of synthesizing thin films using the **sol-gel method**, with a focus on **ZnO (zinc oxide)**. It includes theoretical background, experimental procedure, UV-Vis analysis, and determination of optical properties such as **absorption spectrum** and **bandgap energy**.

---

## 📂 Contents

1. **Introduction**
   - Overview of thin film technology
   - Importance of ZnO films in optoelectronics

2. **Sol-Gel Method**
   - Classification as a chemical deposition method
   - Advantages over physical methods (low cost, low temperature, homogeneity)

3. **Precursors and Stability**
   - Common compounds used (e.g., TEOS, zinc acetate)
   - Factors affecting sol stability (pH, temperature, concentration)

4. **Film Fabrication Process**
   - Hydrolysis and condensation
   - Deposition techniques (e.g., spin coating)
   - Drying and heat treatment steps

5. **Optical Characterization**
   - Absorption spectrum analysis (UV-Vis)
   - Tauc plot construction
   - Bandgap energy estimation for ZnO (~3.2 eV)

6. **Practical Example**
   - Detailed example of solution-based film fabrication using SiO₂ or SnO₂

7. **Appendix**
   - Raw data (.xls)
   - Plotted graphs (absorbance vs wavelength, Tauc plot)

---

## 📈 Key Results

- UV-Vis spectra confirm high absorbance in UV region.
- Bandgap energy of ZnO thin film is approximately **3.2 eV**, consistent with semiconductor behavior.
- Sol-gel method proves effective for producing uniform, transparent thin films.
